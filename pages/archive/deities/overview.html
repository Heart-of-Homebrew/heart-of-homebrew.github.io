Deities of Ceriad

The deities of Ceriad are not vague myths nor distant abstraction. They are physical beings privy to the same forces and laws that govern lesser beings. Each is permitted limited control over certain governing patterns of the universe, with the ability to expand that control as well as their effectiveness in exercising it, improving the rapidness, accuracy, and potency of changes they make.

The Nature of a Deific Soul

A greater soul is the union of a greater spirit and a powerful body capable of bearing it. A deific soul is born when a greater soul is granted deific power, allowing that being to wield forces that can alter the state and functions of reality; Although, even they still face limits and consequences.

The Source of Deific Power

Each deific power enters the world through a physical material (some kind of mineral, substance, or form of matter), Which lies dormant until taken into a deity’s body, where it becomes part of their soul, putting consciousness behind the power.

A dormant power is manifested in its raw, material form, called its "manifestation". When taken into a deity it becomes infused with the soul at a subatomic and subliminal level and becomes completely invisible.

These Powers wax or wane depending on how well their bearers nurture the specific conditions that strengthen them. These conditions are referred to as the powers "sustain".

Physical Space

Ability: The power to distort distance, reshape geometry, or even create impossible places.

Sustain: To Be Determined.

Relations/Connection

Ability: Limited control over the essence of spiritual connection and relationships between souls

Sustain: The existence of more relationships, as well as stronger connections.

Manifestation: To Be Determined.

Soul

Ability: Authority over spirits, their movement, and their passage between planes and in and out of bodies.

Sustain: To Be Determined.

Time Flow

Ability: Influence over entropy, decay, and the alteration of past or future states.

Sustain: The stability of existing timelines, as changes are made at one point or another, those changes have to ripple through the timeline. This means that the more changes that are still settling at a time the less potent the power.

Manifestation: A bright green crystal in many pieces. Each piece is stable on its own

Two anomalous powers also exist. One seeks to consume all things, from bodies, to spirits, to the deific powers themselves. It is wielded by a deity known as Chronos. The other one that seems to disperse such things utterly, scattering them nearly infinitely across the physical plane, and is wielded by a creature unlike any being, referred to as The Destroyer. The nature and origin of these powers remains disputed and feared among the other deities.

Multiplicity & Succession

Powers may be shared under certain conditions, inherited by offspring, split during catastrophes, or even fall dormant due to tragedy. Some deities rise as direct descendants of those before them, like Olruc Ceri and Senek Ceri now sharing the Creation of Life. Others result from stranger fates, such as the divided souls of Chiverous and the unnamed fire-born half who each control half of the Energy Flow. There are also deities who pass with no successor, such as [Space Deity], whose spirit left Ceriad having not passed the power over Physical Space along, causing it to become a dormant power for quite some time; During which time, many artifacts like haversacks and bags of holding started failing.

The deific hosts of Ceriad are not fixed. Deities vanish, ascend, divide, or are overthrown. Alliances shift, rivalries spiral across eons, and even multiple planes of existence. At times, entire deific powers may go quiescent when their bearers falter. Mortals record their legends, but to the godly beings themselves, eternity itself is the record, sometimes whispered, sometimes shouted, but never yet silent.

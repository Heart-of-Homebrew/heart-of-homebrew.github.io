ORIGINAL
THOUGHT

Original Thought is the power to spend energy to conjure unprecedented ideas. The summoned ideas can be shaped and guided through a presented problem needing solved, or through intent and willpower. The rate at which ideas can be summoned depends upon the hosts physical ability to withstand the draw of the power, the energy and focus put into the act, and the weight of the problem, or intention, put behind a use of this power.

The Power to Invent

This power grants its wielder the incredible ability to exchange energy for totally original thoughts. Little is known about the mechanics or components of this power. Some aren't even sure it's current wielder experienced any change upon receiving it. There are many theories surrounding this diffused power. One such that is common among the deities is that because it is so dispersed, it is sewn around and within everything and everyone, meaning that all beings wield a portion of this power.

Known Bearers & Succession

Mondul Opfinder

Mondul Opfinder is known in many continents as a great engineer. Some of the things he has built include: Kibo No Machi, The Mountain Gate to Amalur, and the Effervescan Sky Fleet (??). To show appreciation for all these wonderful creations, John and Chiverous worked together to gather the power of Original Thought and bestow it upon Mondul.

CERI

Ceri was the earliest known deity, and bore the Creation of Life. He is believed to be the source from which all life has sprung forth, from deities to microbes. He is often referred to as "The Father of Life", and his name was bestowed upon the world, Ceriad.

The Passing of the Father

Ceri, the "Father of All", reached a point in his life where he believed he was no longer able to make anything new and didn't have the ability to improve upon his creations any further. To address this, he had many conversations with other deities and great beings. The conclusion was that he needed to find ways to see the world from perspectives other than his own. After contemplating a few ways to do this, he determined the most effective way to do this would be to literally become multiple people.

He accomplished this by creating two clones of himself. He did this because his body was the only one he knew of that could weild his power, but he did not know how to construct another that could. Then, with help from John, the Deity of Souls, Ceri was able to have his power split and shared between the clones; To do away with Ceri for good and leave the world with two entirely new beings in control of the Creation of Life

The two new beings would be Olruc Ceri and Senek Ceri. John was unable to split the power evenly, but rather had to do so by its components, which aren't so rigid or measurable but exist, nonetheless. The components that Olruc received would allow him to create incredibly resilient and refined beings, with limited magical propensity. Senek's components on the other hand would allow his creations to be deeply magical, but have poor physical constitutions.

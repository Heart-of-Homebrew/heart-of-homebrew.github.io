Ceri, the "Father of All", reached a point in his life where he believed he was no longer able to make anything new, and didn't have the ability to improve upon his creations any further. To address this, he had many conversations with other deities and great beings. The conclusion was that he needed to find ways to see the world from perspectives other than his own. After contemplating a few ways to do this, he determined the most effective way to do this would be to literally become multiple people. The two new beings would be Olruc Ceri and Senek Ceri. During the split, Ceri's deific power was also split, to share the power between the two new deities. However, a power doesn't split evenly, but rather by its components, which aren't so rigid or measurable but exist, nonetheless.

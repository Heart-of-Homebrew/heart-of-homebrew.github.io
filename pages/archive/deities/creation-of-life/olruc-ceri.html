Olruc Ceri

Olruc Ceri is one of the two clones of the late Ceri, bearing a portion of the Creation of Life, and inheriting a more precise strain of the power. His portion emphasizes symmetry, organized structure, and resilience, traits he regards as the highest expression of “true beauty.”

The Birth of "The Fair Liar"

Ceri, "The Father of All", created a plan he believed was necessary for the continuity of life on Ceriad. In this plan, he split himself into two beings, who would know eachother as brothers, and know Ceri as their father; Although, they would not have access to Ceri's memories. Olruc was one of these brothers. He had inherited the components of Ceri's power necessary for forming symmetry in living things, which resulted in systemic efficiency that provided an amount of resilience, healing, and endurance to the bodies.

Olruc would take this symmetry as "beauty", and he would lust after it. But deep inside he was envious of the components of his father's power that were passed to his brother, Senek. Without those components he could not create vessels with capacity for deep connection to the spirits they hosted. Thus, they could not wield magic in the same capacity as Senek's creations could. This envy paired poorly with the disgust Olruc had for the physical appearance of, and physiological shortcomings of, his brother's life forms. Olruc grew a deep hatred for Senek, and sought to destroy him.

In the end, Olruc chose to resort to indiscriminate lying, manipulation, and violence toward this end. He committed all manners of taboo against the other deities and beings of Ceriad. This deceitfulness under his guises of "beauty" and "fairness" earned him many of his less flattering names, such as: "The Fair Liar", or [Placeholder].

Symmetry: Strengths and Weaknesses

Olruc is able to form highly symmetrical, structured bodies with stable compositions, predictable development, and high physical endurance. He is also able to take more precise and focused actions with this power. The beings he creates are able to have highly refined bodily systems, giving them vast healing ability and resilience, an efficiency that allows them to work tirelessly compared to other races, as well as higher intellectual function on average.

Yet, while Olruc’s structures are carefully crafted, this strain of the power lacks the forces required to imbue matter with deep spiritual connectivity. As a result, his creations (most notably, the Everian Humans) possess extraordinary physical capability and recovery, but struggle to form strong bonds with their spirits, severely limiting their natural magical potential.

In Fear of Grotesque

Olruc’s long and bitter rivalry with his brother (Ceri's other clone), Senek Ceri, stems from incompatible visions of how life itself should be. Where Olruc values elegance and structural harmony, Senek’s portion of the power fosters dramatic spiritual depth but leaves the resulting forms unpredictable and often asymmetrical, generally chaotic in almost every way.

Olruc had been growing more hateful of Senek steadily since they were both very young. This festered to a point where Olruc decided he would have to do somthing about it. He spent years setting up a plan. He traced down an artifact containing corrupted spirits, that craved nothing but misery and chaos. He set his brother up by convincing him that they should work together to create a really powerful body, but when they conjured it, Olruc imbued the corrupted spirits into the body. The being created went on a destruction spree that wiped out 1/10th of a whole continent.

It was easy for Olruc to convince the other powers of Ceriad that the chaos was a result of Senek's twisted power. After all, Senek had already created a few uncomfortably chaotic things before, but nothing that was nearly as evil as this thing Olruc had him make. Nonetheless, the other deities took Olruc's side, banishing Senek and his creations from the plane Ceriad resided on. However, Senek would make arrangements with the Yrith, the diety of Physical Space, to free himself from exile.

In Pursuit of Beauty

Many of Olruc's deeds along his quest to create "The most beautiful peoples to exist on Ceriad" would be among some of the most hideous usages of deific power in all the records of this archive. He was willing to threaten, blackmail, and even kill people and deities, if it came to it. Chief among his transgressions would be the aforementioned mistreatment of his brother, his controlling of Yrith, and his affair with [Light Deity].

The Everian Humans

Olruc’s most prominent creation is the only full-blown race he has created, known as the Everians. Their hallmark traits reflect his side of the power to create life: remarkable endurance, rapid healing, and a consistent physical symmetry rarely seen in other mortal peoples. But they struggle to learn magic due to their poor connection to their spirits.

This magical limitation proved too debilitating in a highly magical world for the Everians to thrive the way Olruc wanted them to. So he curated a very large basin region, now known as Everia, in the center of the Lamdis contintent. He filled the region with incredibly efficient plant-life, that could grow large very quickly. He even went great lengths to ensure that the region would become a self-sustaining system, essentially an enormous terrarium. He then lied and defrauded to try to ensure his people would never have to be corrupted by the violent, chaotic outside world, cutting them off from Ceriad for centuries.

The Deceiving of Yrith

Olruc did not want his people to be exposed to the chaos of the other peoples in the world, seeing little to no value in any of his fathers creations. So he set his eyes on the power of Physical Space. He knew it could be used to completely conceal the region of Everia by warping the shape of the space surrounding it. However, when he approached Yrith to negotiate having this favor done, the deity of space refused, saying that such a use of his power over such a vast area would have long-lasting negative consequences. Yrith also believed it was unethical to lay such a claim on such a large portion of the shared world of Ceriad.

Seeing that Yrith did not have a people yet, Olruc decided to use that against the other deity. He promised Yrith that if he helped with this, he would be granted a people of his own liking and design. However, Olruc had no intention of following through on this. Yrith, being young, naive, and quite frankly gullible, allowed himself to become convinced it wasn't that bad; He needed a people and was willing to succumb to Olruc's plan in exchange for them.

Three-hundred forty years would go by, All the while Olruc re-iterating that crafting a people takes time and that Yrith needed to be patient. But Yrith was not, especially after his discovery of Senek, and the story behind why the other deity of life was cast out. He tracked down old records written by Aethelian Reliquar to confirm Senek's side of the story was the truth.

Having decided he had played along with Olruc for far too long he reunited the plane of the underworld with the deep underground regions of the world, giving Senek a way back into the plane on which Ceriad resided. Yrith then released the power he was enacting on the space surrounding Everia, causing it to be revealed to the world. Oddly enough, though, nobody noticed. Not right away at least. Yrith would then confide in Mondul Opfinder that he was done being a deity, that he thought such great beings could be beyond such drama but was only ever disappointed. Despite Mondul's earnest efforts to plead with him, Yriths mind was already made up. He proceeded to take his own life and returned his great spirit to the plane of the dead.

Creation of Life

The Creation of Life is the deific power that grants the ability to form bodies capable of housing spirits and becoming souls. Through it, flesh is patterned, vessels are prepared, and entire peoples may be called into being.

The Power to Shape Vessels

With this power a deity can refine inert matter into structures that facilitate organic life and shape those structures into living bodies. But there is a second, and perhaps more important ability this power grants: the ability to transform the very nature of that matter, that the life forms and their offspring may take on a spirit. Bodies with the necessary capacity for greater spirits require a higher level of the powers sustain to be achieved, as well as far more time, energy, and even skill. Young deities who have contentions preventing them from fulfilling their potential, such as Senek and Olruc, would have trouble pulling it off.

The bearer of this power does not create spirits, rather they fashion vessels fit to join with them. In its most common uses, it shapes ordinary mortal peoples and lineages. In rarer cases, and only under very specific circumstances, it forges bodies capable of bearing deific power without being entirely undone by it.

Unfortunately, in recent days this power has been used both nefariously and uncautiously, resulting in certain tragic and potentially catastrophic happenings. One such event resulting in the start of a quiet, but ever-raging battle between the most ultimate forms of growth and decay.

Other Deities' Need for Peoples

In the oldest records, the bearer of Creation of Life is called upon not only to seed their own followers, but to prepare entire peoples on behalf of other deities. This power can be used to tune vessels, and thus the spirits housed in them, toward certain callings, climates, or covenants, so that when another power lays claim to them, their bodies and souls are already shaped to answer. Having followers is often instrumental to any deity looking to bolster their sway in the world of Ceriad, and for some it is a necessary act to sustain their power.

Some nations trace their origin to such covenants, forged when a deity requested a people fit for their patronage and another wielded this power to craft the first bodies of that kind. The details of those arrangements — what was promised, what was owed, and what was broken — remain the shrouded topics of heated dispute among scholars.

Known Bearers & Succession

Tradition holds that Ceri was the only known previous bearer of the Creation of Life and used it to pattern many ancient mortal vessels, and sculpting the current standard way of using the power. In more recent eras, the power is said to be split between Olruc Ceri and Senek Ceri, both clones born of Ceri, Though they are often regarded as his sons and refer to eachother as brothers.

Each of the sons embodies a different strain of this powers potential. Senek bears the will necessary to build strong, sturdy bodies, with much in the way of physical prowess. Olruc, on the other hand, carries the more refined, honed side of the power, which focuses resilience and refinement, or as Olruc himself calls it, "beauty". However, neither carry all that is necessary to realize the full potential of this power: the ability to form a greater soul, a necessary ingredient to bring new deities to life.

Ceri

As you might guess, this late deity's name is the basis for the common name of the world itself: Ceriad. He is the sole creator of all but a few of the current life forms across the world. The exceptions being the "Verminkind", Senek's creation, and the "Everian Humans", Olruc's creation. Even though Ceri was not the direct creator of these, he was, however, the author of the standards by which Olruc and Senek created them. He is, in a sense, in everyone and every living thing.

Olruc Ceri

Olruc inherited a strain of the power of life that emphasizes his ability to create structurally organized beings with even complexions, and highly symmetrical forms, which Olruc sees as more "beautiful", but not everyone is in agreeance with him. While this structurality provides his beings with a particular brand of resilience and strength, he lacks the side of the power required to actually further imbue the matter of his creations with improved metaphysical strength. He created the Everian Humans this way, they are able to heal at extraordinary rates, can live off the land quite easily, require little in the way of comforts and can work very hard for impressively long amounts of time; Though, sadly, their bodies are unable to naturally connect as deeply with their spirits as other beings, hindering their magical abilities and development.

Olruc's relationship with his brother is well beyond fallen out, but even beyond that, Olruc's relations with the other deities of Ceriad have always been strained. He believes he is meant to be the supreme creator of all beings and that his power is the cornerstone of all other powers. He reflects this core belief in the way he treats the other deities, expecting them to bow to him. This causes for much dissent among all the deific powers of the World.

Senek Ceri

Senek inherited the forces necessary to create highly powerful and magical beings, yet none required for intellect or symmetry. Olruc, being disgusted by the misshapenness of these beings, cast Senek into the underworld and trapped him there for a long time. Senek grew sour against Olruc for this, and tried to turn to the other deities for help, but received none because Olruc intervened. Senek, now turned against everyone, set out to create the most powerful race of beings he could conjure and made them as ugly as he possibly could, calling them Verminkind.

The Verminkind reflect all the strengths and weaknesses of the forces he inherited from his father's power: Chronic asymmetry, yet a propensity toward diversification; Low physical intellect, leading to a high tolerance for discomforts and pains; And best of all, a deep connection with their spirits, causing their souls to be incredibly capable of magic. Even though their low intellect makes their use of it less inventive, typically, it is still incredibly influential.

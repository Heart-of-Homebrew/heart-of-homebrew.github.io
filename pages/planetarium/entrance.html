Planetarium

A first person experience with celestial bodies, star charts, and cosmic movements as documented by the scholars of the Athenaeum.

Upon completion of the Planetarium's construction, You will be able to view and interact with celestial bodies across multiple planes, and timelines.

Note: This wing will require the construction of the Cartohall's Sanctum of the Globe first, as a major function of it will be the ability to transport you to a selected celestial body's globe within the Sanctum.

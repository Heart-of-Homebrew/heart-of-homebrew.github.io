Athenaeum Ceriad

A living compendium of the world of Ceriad | maps, lore, and living history

Ceriad was once much like our own world, with blue seas, snow-capped mountains, and everything between, but now its equator is literally ablaze while its poles seem eternally frozen. It is shaped not only by tectonics and weather but by deities whose whims can lean upon the very laws of physics; the gods here are physical forces, able to bend the rules of nature so that storms, miracles, and strange wonders can all be part of an ordinary life.

And yet its lands hold many familiar peoples and cultures, from cliff-dwelling dwarves to seafaring powers to human farming colonies. People both help and harm each other, both give and take, and can seldom agree on what’s sacred, yet none escape the touch of that which is. Above all, Ceriad is a chronicle in motion, a great, living compendium of rival civilizations, powerful deities, long prayers, and short memories, where every graveyard an archive and every coastline an atlas page, all hinting that the world is far older, vaster, and more tightly woven than any single mind can grasp.

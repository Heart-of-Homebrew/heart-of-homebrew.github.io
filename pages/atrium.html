Athenaeum Ceriad

A vast library of the world of Ceriad | maps, lore, and living history

Ceriad is much like our own world, with blue seas and snow-capped mountains, but its equator is ablaze and its poles eternally frozen. It is shaped not only by tectonics and weather but by deities whose whims can alter the very laws of physics; deities that have the power to bend the rules of nature such that calamity, miracles, and strange wonders are far too commonplace. It's home to familiar peoples and cultures, from cave dwelling foragers to naval superpowers. They both help and hurt each other, both give and take, and seldom agree on what’s sacred.

Above all, Ceriad is a breathing chronicle, a living tome, of rival nations, powerful deities, long prayers, and short memories, where every graveyard an archive and every coastline an atlas page, all hinting that it is far older, vaster, and more tightly woven than any single mind can grasp.
